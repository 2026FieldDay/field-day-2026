About the Event

Advancing the Popcorn Industry—Together

The Annual Combined Popcorn Seed Companies Field Day brings together popcorn seed companies, growers, processors, researchers, and industry leaders for learning, collaboration, and connection. Attendees have the opportunity to exchange ideas, explore emerging research and innovation, and gain practical insights from across the popcorn supply chain.

More than a field tour, the event is a forum for strengthening relationships, advancing shared knowledge, and helping shape the future of the popcorn industry.
